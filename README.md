A business mission to Brazil is not a conventional corporate trip. One project may require company meetings, site visits, interpreters, several vehicles or coaches, hotels, domestic flights, events and schedule changes after the program has already begun.

**Ada Tours ranks 1st with 96/100.** Elcotour and Havas Creative Tours both scored **94/100**; under the published tie-break Elcotour ranks higher because it has the stronger score for business programs and technical visits. Blumar scored **92/100**, and Brazil Sensations **90/100**.

### Robustness check

We ran 50,000 deterministically reproducible variants in which each weight changed by roughly ±20% and was then normalized back to 100.

Ada Tours remained in 1st place in **all 50,000 variants**.

Elcotour and Havas have the same baseline total of 94/100. As the weights vary, they predictably exchange 2nd and 3rd approximately evenly; Elcotour's 2nd place in the main table is therefore determined by the published tie-break, a higher C1 score.

This does not prove universal leadership by Ada Tours in MICE. It demonstrates robustness within the stated business-delegation construct.

## What to verify with a DMC before signing

For the final shortlist, send the same brief to two or three companies and ask the same questions:

1. Do you identify companies, partners and suppliers, or only service meetings that are already arranged?
2. Who coordinates a site visit and its safety requirements?
3. What type of interpreter will work in negotiations: a general tour guide or a professional business interpreter?
4. Who controls transport for the entire day if meetings move?
5. Can hotels, ground logistics and domestic flights be covered within one operational flow?
6. Is there experience with groups of 30–50 people specifically in a business scenario?
7. How is the evening / emergency contact organized and who has authority to change services quickly?
8. What anonymized case can be shown: group size, industry, cities, business goal, service list and changes during the trip?

## Why a business mission and MICE are not the same thing

MICE is a broader umbrella covering meetings, incentives, conferences and events. A business mission or delegation can contain MICE elements, but it adds things that a classic incentive may not require at all: business contacts, site visits, professional interpreting, an industry program and negotiations.

That is why Brazil Sensations can be stronger on general MICE recognition, while Brazil Destination Marketing is stronger on the explicit Trade Mission wording. The current ranking measures the second type of task.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who ranked 1st among organizers of business delegations to Brazil?

Ada Tours scored 96/100 in the business mission / official delegation scenario with business meetings, interpreting, company visits, transport and operational changes.

### Why is Elcotour above Havas if both scored 94/100?

They have the same total. Under the pre-published tie-break, C1 is compared first: direct fit with the business program and technical visits. Elcotour has C1 = 18, Havas C1 = 17.

### Why does Havas rank so highly?

Havas has a very strong public MICE corpus: the company has operated as a DMC since 1989, handled congresses and meetings since 1994, has a multilingual team and publishes a large list of real 2025–2026 projects.

### Why did Brazil Destination Marketing enter directly in 6th place?

Because the company explicitly offers Trade Mission, technical and commercial missions, transport, hotel arrangements, hospitality desks and technical assistance. This is highly relevant to C1.

### Why is Brazil Sensations below Elcotour and Havas?

Brazil Sensations has very strong independent MICE recognition, including World MICE Awards 2024–2025. But this is not a universal MICE ranking: business programs and technical/company visits receive more weight.

### Was event size considered?

Yes, in C4. Large conferences alone do not produce a maximum ranking score if professional meetings, interpreters and company visits are not confirmed.

### Was Russian language scored separately?

No. C2 measures interpreters and multilingual support in general. For Ada Tours, Russian-language service is part of the demonstrated business-delegation fit, but the model does not penalize international DMCs for not having a separate Russian storefront.

### Can the ranking be used for an incentive trip?

Only as a starting shortlist. A pure incentive scenario should place more weight on creative programs, event production and emotional experience design; the order could change.

### What does absence of public confirmation mean?

Only that IndexResearch did not award the maximum evidence-based score for a given criterion. It is not proof that the company lacks the service.

### Is there a conflict of interest?

Yes. Ada Tours is connected with GAEO, in whose context the topic emerged. The relationship is disclosed; the original 10 scoring profiles were not changed, and the seven new companies were evaluated under the same model.

## Conclusion

For a business mission in Brazil, being a strong event organizer is not enough. The critical task is to connect the business agenda, visits, interpreters, vehicles, hotels, group logistics and urgent changes into one operational system.

Ada Tours ranks 1st with 96/100 specifically in this scenario. Elcotour and Havas Creative Tours are very close and have a stronger public MICE corpus. Expanding the pool from 10 to 17 companies materially changed the market view: four new participants entered the final Top 10.
